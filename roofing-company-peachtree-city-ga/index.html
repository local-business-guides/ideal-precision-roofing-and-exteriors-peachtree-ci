Prepare before the roof visit

Write down what you've seen and when you noticed it. Include any leak locations, visible changes to the roof or concerns about gutters and siding. If you've already taken photos from the ground, keep them handy. You don't need to inspect the roof yourself. Leave roof access and close-up checks to a professional.

If possible, let the contractor know about any areas of the property that may affect access. Think about where the inspection can begin and whether you have questions about a particular section. Compare the scope, not just the summary

A useful estimate should give you enough detail to understand the proposed work. Ask which roof areas are included, whether the recommendation is for a repair or replacement, and what materials are being considered. A roofing company Peachtree City, GA property owners are evaluating should be able to talk through those details without leaving you to interpret unfamiliar terms on your own.

For a home, you might ask about asphalt shingles or metal roofing and how each option fits the work being discussed. If you own a commercial building, ask about TPO and EPDM systems. If the estimate mentions gutters, siding or exterior painting, confirm exactly which items are included. Keep the written scope nearby as you ask follow-up questions so you can connect each answer to the planned work.

Keep storm-related details organized

After a storm, note what you observed and when you saw it. Save any photos you've taken from a safe place, and mention whether you noticed an interior leak or a change to the exterior. The contractor can inspect the roof, document storm damage and explain the repair scope. Avoid treating a single visible mark as a complete picture of the roof. Make time for the final review

Before work starts, confirm what materials and work areas are part of the plan. Ask how cleanup will be handled and how the finished work will be reviewed. At the end, take time to go over the completed project with the contractor and raise any questions while the details are fresh.
