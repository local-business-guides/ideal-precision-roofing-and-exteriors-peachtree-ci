Questions to ask before scheduling

A few direct questions can help you understand the process and decide what information you still need. Consider asking:

  • Which credentials or approvals apply to my specific project?
  • Where can I verify those details?
  • What roof areas will you inspect?
  • What materials and work are included in the estimate?
  • How will storm damage, if present, be recorded?

Listen for clear answers rather than relying on a title alone. If the work includes gutters, siding, or exterior painting along with roofing, ask which parts are covered in the proposed scope. When something is unclear, request an explanation before making a decision.

Read the estimate with care

A useful estimate should help you understand the recommended work, not leave you guessing about what happens next. Review whether it describes a repair or replacement, identifies the materials being considered, and explains the areas involved. Ask what is excluded, too. That can prevent confusion if you expect a gutter or siding concern to be included but the document only addresses the roof.

For a roof replacement, ask how the contractor will handle installation, site cleanup, and the final review. If a repair is proposed, ask why that approach fits the observed condition. Write down any leaks, stains, or changes you’ve noticed, along with when they first appeared. You can take photos from a safe place, but don’t climb onto the roof to collect them. If you’re asking about storm damage, mention when the weather event occurred and point out any visible concerns from the ground.
