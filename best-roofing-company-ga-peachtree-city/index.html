Questions worth asking before work begins

Ask what parts of the roof were inspected, what the contractor found, and which items need attention now. If replacement is being discussed, ask what material is proposed and how the contractor arrived at that recommendation. You can also ask what the repair scope includes, what areas might need extra attention, and how cleanup and the final review are handled.

A useful estimate should make the planned work understandable. If a recommendation is unclear, ask for it to be explained before you agree to proceed. A quick checklist for comparing roofers

Before choosing, take a few minutes to check that you can answer these questions:

  • Did the inspection identify specific roof conditions?
  • Can the contractor explain why each repair or replacement is recommended?
  • Are the materials and planned work described clearly?
  • Have you discussed installation, cleanup, and the final review?
  • Do you know who to contact if you have a question about the estimate?

Comparing these details can be more helpful than relying on a broad claim that a roofer is the best. Look for a proposal that connects the observed condition to the work being suggested.

Mistakes to avoid when hiring

Try not to make a decision based on a quick glance at the roof or on a recommendation you do not understand. A storm can leave damage that is difficult to see from the ground, while a concern that looks serious may need a more focused inspection to determine its scope. Avoid agreeing to work before you know what the contractor plans to repair or replace.

Before an inspection, make a note of leaks, missing or damaged shingles, or other concerns you have noticed. Share when you first saw the issue and whether it changed after a storm.
